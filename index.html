Subject-Verb Agreement

Singular subjects need singular verbs. Plural subjects need plural verbs.

❌ The group of students are studying.

✅ The group of students is studying. (Group is singular)

Run-on Sentences

Two independent sentences squished together without proper punctuation.

❌ I love writing it is my favorite subject.

✅ I love writing. It is my favorite subject.
