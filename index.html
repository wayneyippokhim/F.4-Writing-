article Sentence patterns used in a feature article expand_more

Describing the problem

You may find it difficult to …

E.g. You may find it difficult to keep practicing for four days a week.

E.g. You may find it difficult to wake up at 6:30 every morning.

…(n.)… can be quite annoying, especially when …SVO…

E.g. Practicing for four days a week can be quite annoying, especially when you don’t like what you’re doing.

E.g. Waking up at 6:30 every morning can be quite annoying, especially when you had a long night.

Showing empathy

It sounds like you’re going through a really tough time.

That sounds incredibly frustrating.

Giving suggestions

If I were you, I would …(v.)…

E.g. If I were you, I would talk to my coach seriously.

E.g. If I were you, I would sleep earlier the night before.

For dealing with the situation, it is important to …(v.)…

E.g. For dealing with the situation, it is important to let your coach know about your difficulty.

E.g. For dealing with the situation, it is important to set a timetable.

One of the suggested methods, which you can try, is to …(v.)…

E.g. One of the suggested methods, which you can try, is to reduce the number of practices.

volunteer_activism Sentence patterns used in a letter of advice expand_more

Pointing out a problem

The main challenge you may face is that …

E.g. The main challenge that you may face is that the music industry is extremely competitive, and dropping out of school now means you lose a vital backup plan.

It is clear that there is a drawback with …

E.g. It is clear that there is a drawback with leaving school early, as you wont have the diploma you need if your singing career doesn’t work well.

Giving suggestions

It is highly recommended that …

E.g. It is highly recommended that you should search part-time jobs in the music industry to contribute to your career knowledge.

A practical solution to this problem would be to …

E.g. A practical solution to this problem would be to ask your parents if you can spend every evening after homework performing live streams to build your audience.
