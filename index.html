Simple Sentences (SV / SVO)

Subject + Verb: The dog barked.

Subject + Verb + Object: The student wrote an essay.

Compound Sentences (FANBOYS)

Join two independent clauses with a comma and a conjunction (For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So).

Example: I wanted to study, but I was too tired.

Complex Sentences

Join an independent clause with a dependent clause using words like although, because, since, or if.

Example: Because it was raining, we stayed indoors.

Subject-Verb Agreement

Singular subjects need singular verbs. Plural subjects need plural verbs.

❌ The group of students are studying.

✅ The group of students is studying. (Group is singular)

Run-on Sentences

Two independent sentences squished together without proper punctuation.

❌ I love writing it is my favorite subject.

✅ I love writing. It is my favorite subject.
